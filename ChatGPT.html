Ethel Cain is the artistic project of American singer-songwriter Hayden Anhedönia, known for immersive songwriting, atmospheric production, and a distinct vision shaped by Southern Gothic imagery.

Her music moves through memory, religion, family, isolation, and the complicated tenderness of being human. Each song feels like a letter discovered in an abandoned house — intimate, unsettling, and impossible to forget.

This is music for the long drive home, the last light on an empty road, and the things we don't know how to say out loud.
